# Beginner Grammar & Common Indian English Tips

## 1. Self-Introductions: Avoiding Common Mistakes
- **Common Mistake**: Saying "Myself Ramesh" or "Myself Rahul".
- **Correct Usage**: "My name is Ramesh" or "I am Ramesh".
- **Tip**: In formal and informal English, always use "My name is..." or "I'm..." when introducing yourself.

## 2. Using Tenses Correctly
- **Past Events**: Use simple past tense for completed actions (e.g., "I completed my project yesterday", NOT "I have complete my project").
- **Ongoing Practice**: Use present continuous for actions happening now (e.g., "I am preparing for an interview").
- **Habits & Routines**: Use simple present tense (e.g., "I practice English every evening").

## 3. Preposition Pitfalls
- **Discussing**: Say "Let's discuss the project" (DO NOT say "discuss about").
- **Ordering**: Say "I ordered food" (DO NOT say "ordered for food").
- **Reaching**: Say "I reached the venue" (DO NOT say "reached to the venue").

## 4. Asking Questions Politely
- Use modal verbs like "Could you please repeat that?" or "May I ask a question?" instead of abrupt phrases.
