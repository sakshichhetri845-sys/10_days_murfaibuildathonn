# College Presentations & Campus Communication

## 1. Classroom & Seminar Presentations
- **Opening Slide**: "Hello everyone! Today, my team and I will present on..."
- **Transitioning Speakers**: "Now, I would like to hand over to my teammate Pooja to discuss the methodology."
- **Concluding**: "Thank you for your time. We are now open to any questions."

## 2. Communicating with Professors & Mentors
- **Email/In-Person**: "Respected Sir/Ma'am, I am writing to seek your guidance regarding my seminar topic."
- **Requesting Extension/Permission**: "Could I please request permission to submit the report by tomorrow?"

## 3. Participating in Group Discussions (GD)
- **Initiating**: "I'd like to initiate the discussion by defining the core topic..."
- **Politely Agreeing/Disagreeing**: "I agree with your point, and to add to that..." or "I see your perspective, but another factor to consider is..."
