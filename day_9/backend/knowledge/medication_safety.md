# General Medication Safety Principles

## Safe Usage Guidelines
- Always follow prescribed dosages and doctor or pharmacist instructions.
- Never share prescription medications with others.
- Store medications in a cool, dry place away from children.
- Check expiration dates regularly.

## Reminders & Adherence
- Take medications at consistent times each day.
- Use scheduled voice reminders or pill organizers if helpful.
- If you miss a dose, check the product packaging instructions or speak to a pharmacist before doubling up.
