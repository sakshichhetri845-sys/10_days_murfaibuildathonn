# Urgent Care & Warning Signs Guidance

## Critical Emergency Warning Signs
If you or someone around you experiences any of the following, seek immediate emergency medical care:
- Severe difficulty breathing or shortness of breath.
- Persistent chest pain or pressure.
- Sudden numbness, weakness, or facial drooping.
- Sudden loss of vision, speech, or balance.
- Severe, uncontrollable bleeding.
- Loss of consciousness or sudden confusion.

## HealthSathi Safety Protocol
- HealthSathi does NOT diagnose medical conditions or prescribe treatments.
- In any emergency situation, prioritize calling local emergency services or reaching a hospital immediately.
