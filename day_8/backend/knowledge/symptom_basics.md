# Common Symptom Basics & Information Guidance

## Fever
- A fever is generally a body temperature above 100.4°F (38°C).
- Rest, stay hydrated, and monitor temperature.
- Consult a doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days, exceeds 103°F (39.4°C), or is accompanied by severe headache, stiff neck, or difficulty breathing.

## Headache
- Common types include tension headaches, migraines, and dehydration headaches.
- Drink water, rest in a quiet, dark room, and manage stress.
- Seek medical attention if a headache comes on suddenly and severely ("thunderclap headache"), or follows a head injury.

## Cough and Cold
- Usually caused by viral infections and resolve within 7 to 10 days.
- Warm liquids, honey (for adults), steam inhalation, and rest help soothe symptoms.
- Contact a doctor if cough persists over 3 weeks, produces blood, or is accompanied by chest pain.

## Fatigue
- Persistent tiredness can result from poor sleep, stress, nutritional gaps, or underlying conditions.
- Maintain consistent sleep schedules and balanced hydration.
- Consult a doctor if fatigue is unexplainable, severe, or long-lasting.
