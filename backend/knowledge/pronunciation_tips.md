# Pronunciation & Speech Clarity Tips for Indian Learners

## 1. Distinguishing 'V' and 'W' Sounds
- **'V' Sound**: Place upper teeth lightly on lower lip and vibrate voice (e.g., *Very*, *Voice*, *Value*).
- **'W' Sound**: Round your lips into an 'O' shape (e.g., *Work*, *Water*, *Welcome*).
- **Practice Tip**: Contrast "Very" vs "Wary", "Vine" vs "Wine".

## 2. Distinguishing 'P' and 'F' Sounds
- **'P' Sound**: Press both lips together and pop air out (e.g., *Paper*, *Project*).
- **'F' Sound**: Upper teeth on lower lip blowing smooth air out (e.g., *First*, *Feedback*).

## 3. Mastering the 'TH' Sound
- **Voiced 'TH'**: Tongue lightly between teeth (e.g., *This*, *That*, *There*).
- **Unvoiced 'TH'**: Soft breath out (e.g., *Think*, *Thank*, *Three*).

## 4. Overcoming MTI (Mother Tongue Influence) Anxiety
- Speak slightly slower to give your tongue and brain time to articulate words clearly.
- Natural clarity and confidence are much more important than trying to mimic foreign accents!
