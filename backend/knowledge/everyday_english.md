# Everyday English & Casual Conversations in India

## 1. Daily Small Talk
- **Greeting Friends & Colleagues**: "Hey! How's your day going?" or "Hi! Good to see you."
- **Asking About Weekend/Routine**: "What are your plans for the weekend?" or "How was your day at college/work?"

## 2. Navigating Daily Situations in India
- **Auto / Cab / Travel**: "Could you please take me to the metro station?" or "How long will it take to reach?"
- **Ordering Food / Cafe**: "Could I get a coffee, please?" or "Could you make it less spicy?"
- **Shopping & Markets**: "How much is this?" or "Do you have this in a different size?"

## 3. Asking for Clarification Gracefully
- If you didn't hear: "Pardon me, could you please repeat that?"
- If you didn't understand: "Could you please explain that once more?" or "I didn't quite catch that."
